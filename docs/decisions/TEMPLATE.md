## Context

What forced a decision. Constraints that matter. What was true at the time.

## Decision

What we are doing. Be specific enough that someone can implement it.

## Rejected alternatives

What else was considered and the reason it lost. This is the part future readers
actually need — without it, the decision looks arbitrary and gets re-litigated.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it commits us to. Include the
things that will bite later.
