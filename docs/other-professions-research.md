# Other professions: research

Unio fits project professions, not ongoing ones. Linked from [future-ideas.md](future-ideas.md), "Other professions".

Research date: 30 Oct 2026. Sources are cited inline. Where a figure comes from a vendor writing about its own competitors, an undated page or a search snippet, the text says so. The reader does not need to be a software engineer: technical terms are explained where they first appear.

## Executive summary

**Unio can serve some of the professions on the list well, and most of them poorly.** It fits the professions that work in **projects with stages and an end**: interior designers, landscape architects (especially those who are architects), and offices that design structures and building systems (structural, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, fire safety). Their work already runs on the same steps as architecture: briefing, preliminary study, detailed drawings, approvals, follow-up during construction. Unio's current features (client page, stages, proposals with online acceptance, versioned files in Drive) cover most of what they need. Event planners and consulting-style law firms come next, with moderate fit. Accountants, psychologists, nutritionists, personal trainers and photographers fit poorly. Their work is ongoing (monthly or per session) or built around a different core tool (meal-plan calculator, workout library, photo-selection gallery, tax software). Each of those markets already has cheap, mature Brazilian tools priced between free and about R$ 140 a month. Health professions also bring LGPD "sensitive data" duties and very long record-keeping rules. **Recommended order: interior designers first, then design-engineering offices, with event planners as an optional experiment. Do not pursue health, accounting or photography.** The team's earlier sketch in `docs/future-ideas.md` mostly holds up. One change matters: the evidence puts **client uploads, approval of individual files and outside collaborators** ahead of the open-ended `lifecycle`, measurements and tasks. Those three serve the professions Unio should pursue first. `lifecycle` and measurements mainly serve the professions it should avoid.

## The professions that share architecture's shape

### Interior designers: the closest match, and the largest nearby market with a count

Interior design runs on the same steps as architecture. A briefing captures goals, palette, materials and style ([Educa Mais Brasil](https://www.educamaisbrasil.com.br/cursos-e-faculdades/design-de-interiores/noticias/quais-as-etapas-de-um-projeto-de-design-de-interiores)). A **preliminary study** follows: floor-plan layout plus 3D views of each room. That wording comes from a project specification written for architecture, not for interiors specifically ([CRC-PR spec](https://www.crcpr.org.br/data/conteudo/2-5%20especificacoes%20propostas%20adicionais%20-%2038-2023.pdf)). The **detailed project** has furnished plans, ceiling plans, electrical and plumbing points, joinery details, finishes and lighting specifications, and follow-up during the works ([CIDESP](https://cidesp.com.br/conteudo/tabela-de-precos-de-design-de-interiores-guia-completo)). Fees are usually quoted from the ABD fee table or per square metre. CIDESP's figure of R$ 20–80/m² for a concept-only project has no date or method, so treat it with caution ([CIDESP](https://cidesp.com.br/conteudo/tabela-de-precos-de-design-de-interiores-guia-completo)).

The market is sizeable: **38,142 active companies** registered under the interior-design activity code (CNAE 7410-2/02), plus 1,030 branches, as of June 2022 ([Sebrae PR](https://sebraepr.com.br/comunidade/artigo/sebrae-em-dados-designer-de-interiores)). Search summaries of the same Sebrae page say 5,245 opened in 2021 and 69% use the simplified tax regime, but that part of the page was not read directly. Abroad, interior designers pay for studio suites built around project phases, a product specification book, purchasing and a **client portal in which the client approves moodboards and product selections item by item** ([Agiled](https://agiled.app/blog/best-client-management-software-for-interior-designers); [HoneyBook](https://honeybook.com/interior-design-business-software)). Those suites cost US$ 59 per user per month (Programa) up to US$ 99–399 per month (Houzz Pro), with sources disagreeing on Houzz Pro's plans ([DesignFiles on Programa](https://blog.designfiles.co/programa-design-software/); [ToolRadar](https://toolradar.com/compare/houzz-pro-vs-planify); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/199689/Houzz-Pro/pricing/)). **No Brazilian equivalent was found.** The foreign suites are priced in dollars, are not in Portuguese, and are built around US trade-pricing habits. The Brazilian commission practice known as "reserva técnica" works differently, though the research found no source describing it.

Regulation does not get in the way. Lei 13.369/2016 recognises the profession but creates no council to register with or answer to ([Planalto](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_Ato2015-2018/2016/Lei/L13369.htm); [Confea opinion](https://consultapublica.confea.org.br/anexos/Anteprojeto002-2020/ParecerTecnico.pdf)). CAU Resolução 51/2013 reserves some interior work for architects, and the designers' association (ABD) challenged it at the Supreme Court ([Sedep](https://www.sedep.com.br/?p=68679)). One partisan source says the court upheld the CAU rules, but that was not verified ([ES Brasil](https://esbrasil.com.br/valorizacao-dos-arquitetos/)). For Unio the point is simple. Architects who do interiors already fit, and non-architect designers need stage names they can change, which Unio already allows through custom project types.

**What is missing** is narrow. Unio needs approval and comments on each file or version (layout v2 approved, 3D v1 needs changes), and a **product or shopping list** the client approves item by item. Whether Brazilian interior designers pay for any management tool today, and whether their clients expect a portal rather than WhatsApp plus a Drive folder, is unknown. That is the first thing to check with real offices.

### Landscape architects: easy when they are architects, unclear otherwise

No source described a landscape workflow, so the stage list (survey, preliminary study, preliminary design, detailed design, planting) is a reasonable guess, not a finding. The profession is contested. CAU counts landscape architecture among architects' areas ([NormasBrasil](https://www.normasbrasil.com.br/norma/resolucao-51-2013_256546.html)). The technicians' council gave landscape technicians the right to prepare and carry out landscape projects in 2023 ([CRT-SP](https://www.crtsp.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/RESOLUCAO-CFT-No-248_2023_TECNICOS-EM-PAISAGISMO.pdf)). The engineering council is defining agronomists' rights in the same area ([Confea](https://www.confea.org.br/midias/2025-12/SEI_1393007_Proposta_19.pdf)), and a bill to regulate the profession has been debated ([Câmara](https://www.camara.leg.br/noticias/416870-regulamentacao-da-profissao-de-paisagista-divide-opinioes-em-debate/)). An office led by an architect is, for Unio, an architecture office with different project types. It needs nothing new beyond a plant list, which is the same feature as the interiors shopping list. Landscapers who are not architects form a separate, fragmented group with no count found.

### Design-engineering offices: strong fit, few competitors, and a ready-made channel

Offices that design structures and building systems (structural, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, fire safety) follow the same national standard vocabulary as architects. NBR 16636 names the stages survey, needs programme, feasibility, preliminary study, preliminary design, legal project, basic project and project for execution. It explicitly includes preliminary studies and detailed projects for these "complementary" disciplines. The sources are teaching copies, not the ABNT text ([Gran Cursos](https://questoes.grancursosonline.com.br/questoes-de-concursos/arquitetura-nbr-16636-1-elaboracao-e-desenvolvimento-de-servicos-tecnicos-especializados-de-projetos-arquitetonicos-e-urbanisticos-parte-1-diretrizes-e-terminologia-429884); [USP copy](https://edisciplinas.usp.br/pluginfile.php/5093027/mod_resource/content/2/NBR16636-2%2C%20gerado%20em%2007_03_2018.pdf)). Their rhythm is set by **coordination rounds**: at the preliminary study, at the preliminary design and again in the detailed phases. In each round every discipline sends its model, clashes are found, corrections go back and the result is checked again ([Soluções CAD/BIM](https://solucoescad.com.br/guia-de-compatibilizacao-de-projetos-bim/)). Deliverables are numbered revisions (R0, R1, R2…), models (IFC, RVT) and clash reports ([AsBEA](https://www.asbea.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/BIM2.pdf)). In other words: stages, versioned files, a proposal and meetings, which is Unio's model.

Every engineering contract needs an **ART** (Anotação de Responsabilidade Técnica, the engineer's registered statement of responsibility) filed with the state CREA. The 2026 fee is **R$ 108.39 for contracts up to R$ 15,000 and R$ 285.59 above** ([Crea-PR](https://www.crea-pr.org.br/ws/art-anotacao-de-responsabilidade-tecnica/valores-de-taxas/); [Crea-MG](https://www.crea-mg.org.br/node/2677)). Owners and city halls ask for it, and architects need every engineer's ART for the approval package. Architects file the equivalent RRT with CAU, so one "technical responsibility" record type would serve both. The construction-site diary was revoked as a council obligation in 2023 and is now good practice or a contract term, except in public works ([Confea](https://www.confea.org.br/plenario-do-confea-revoga-livro-de-ordem-de-obras-e-servicos); [Sienge](https://www.sienge.com.br/blog/diario-de-obra-obrigatoriedade-confea/)).

The pool is large. The Confea/Crea system has about **1.2 million registered professionals, about 39% (roughly 470k) in civil engineering** ([Direito Global, Sep 2026](https://direitoglobal.com.br/2026/09/17/brasil-tem-12-milhao-de-engenheiros/)). The number of engineering firms was not found in free sources. The software market is crowded on the construction-site side and on document control for construction companies. Small design offices have no client-facing tool: **no product aimed at a 2–10 person structural or MEP office's clients was found.** Construction-site tools cost from R$ 269/month (Mais Controle) and R$ 399.20/month (Obra Prima, 2 sites, 1 user). The figures come from a comparison written by a competitor, Vobi, and the client portal is often a paid add-on or top-tier feature ([Vobi comparison](https://www.vobi.com.br/blog/os-melhores-softwares-de-gestao-de-obras-em-2026); [Obra Prima](https://obraprima.eng.br/planos/)). Document-control tools such as Construcode publish no price, and they treat numbered revisions, approval states ("under review, approved, released") and permissions by company as standard ([Construcode](https://site.construcode.com.br/pricing/)). Complaints about Sienge and Vobi centre on surprise charges, slow support and hard cancellation ([Reclame Aqui Sienge](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/softplan-poligraph/cobranca-indevida-por-funcionalidade-prometida-como-gratuita-no-sistema-sienge-softplan-e-suporte-demorado_5oNMeDyOOKeHUTk3/); [Reclame Aqui Vobi](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/home-hero/falha-na-prestacao-do-servico-vobi-sistema-inacessivel-cobranca-indevida-e-dificuldade-no-cancelamento_v_bMfSmHW90dvF_P/)). Simple, flat pricing could stand out.

The engineer's client is often not the owner. Architecture contracts sometimes include the structural, electrical and plumbing projects as items, so **the architect is the engineer's client** ([Gera Contratos](https://geracontratos.com.br/modelo-contrato-arquitetura)). That opens an acquisition channel. Each architecture project involves several engineering disciplines. If an architect on Unio can invite "Fulano Engenharia, structural" into a project to upload revisions and the ART, each paying architect shows Unio to several engineering offices. Document platforms already let many companies' people into one project: Construcode plans count 50 to 100 people per project, with permissions by company ([Construcode](https://site.construcode.com.br/pricing/)). No data exists on how often such guests become paying customers in Brazil.

**What is missing for engineers:** a way for clients and partners to send files in (architectural base drawings, soil tests, surveys, comments from city hall or the fire brigade); ART/RRT records shown on the client page; revision labels and an "issued for approval / issued for construction" status; a pending-issues list for coordination rounds; and outside collaborators inside a project. Viewing CAD and BIM files in the browser would help but is not essential. **Blockers:** BIM files can be very large, and some offices keep files on servers or Autodesk's cloud rather than Google Drive. Construction-site management (daily logs, measurements, schedules, purchasing) is a separate product category with many cheap tools from R$ 34/month ([Dia de Obra](https://diadeobra.com.br/); prices from search snippets). Unio should only offer the client-reporting tip of it.

## Professions that fit in shape but not in budget or data

### Event planners: a countdown project with little software, and small tickets

Wedding and event planning is a project with an end: the event date. That fits Unio's stages, meetings (tastings, visits, rehearsals) and especially **proposals with versions**, since quotes change often. Couples already hold their own consumer tools: a 100+ item checklist and a supplier list with private notes and prices ([Casamentos.com.br](https://www.casamentos.com.br/artigos/conheca-as-ferramentas-do-meu-casamento--c5275)). Planners improvise with generic tools. One example is a Notion template for planners sold from R$ 99 ([Gumroad](https://essentialnotion.gumroad.com/l/essential-cerimonialist-pro)), and ClickUp publishes Portuguese wedding checklists ([ClickUp](https://clickup.com/pt-BR/blog/444500/modelos-de-lista-de-verificacao-de-casamento)). **No Brazilian business tool for planners with public prices was found.** That is a weak signal of a gap, not proof. The event chain is worth **R$ 813.5 billion a year (4.6% of GDP)** and is described as fragmented ([IstoÉ Dinheiro, May 2026](https://istoedinheiro.com.br/eventos-emprega-127-mi-46-pib-7526)). Planners are a small, mostly informal slice of it, with no headcount. São Paulo planners advertise services **from R$ 600–900** ([Estações](https://www.casamentos.com.br/cerimonialista/estacoes-cerimonial-e-assessoria--e428287); [CheckListsp](https://www.casamentos.com.br/cerimonialista/checklistsp-solucoes-em-eventos--e146438)), which caps what they would pay for software. An academic design for a Brazilian wedding tool used three kinds of user (couple, suppliers, planner) and a budget with spent and remaining amounts ([UNIFEV](https://periodicos.unifev.edu.br/revistaunifev/article/view/288/313)). Unio would need a supplier list, a budget, a guest list and a day-of schedule. The supplier list overlaps with the interiors shopping list.

### Brand and graphic designers: rounds of feedback on specific files

A brand project runs as a briefing, then round 1 (one or two directions), round 2 (refine one), round 3 (final), then the brand manual, delivered after the final payment. Extra revision rounds are charged ([Contra](https://contra.com/s/N3OlMkWY-branding-design); [Contra logo](https://contra.com/s/ZABMPYql-logo-basic)). The provider writes the proposal, scope and schedule, and the client approves values and payment terms before work starts ([Sebrae MG](https://sebrae.com.br/Sebrae/Portal%20Sebrae/UFs/MG/Sebrae%20de%20A%20a%20Z/Branding-DC22001-1.pdf)). Unio's proposals and file versions fit. What is missing is **approval and comments on each file**, plus ongoing retainer work (monthly social-media pieces). No Brazilian source on retainers was found. Designers combine Milanote (from US$ 9.99/month), HoneyBook (US$ 29–129) or Dubsado (US$ 35–55) ([Subger](https://subger.com/en/service/milanote); [Assembly](https://assembly.com/blog/honeybook-pricing); [Agiled](https://agiled.app/blog/dubsado-pricing)). Filestage's file-approval tool starts at US$ 199/month ([PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/filestage)), which shows agencies value per-file approval but solo designers will not pay that much. CAGED records 31,421 hires plus dismissals of graphic designers over 12 months ([Salario.com.br](https://www.salario.com.br/profissao/desenhista-industrial-grafico-designer-grafico-cbo-262410/)). That is a sense of scale, not a count of offices. Brand designers become a by-product once per-file approval exists for interiors and engineers, but are not worth a dedicated push.

### Lawyers: a client page fits consulting work; litigation depends on court data

A consulting job (a contract, an opinion, a property deal) looks like an architecture project: proposal, deliverable, versions. A lawsuit can run for years with long quiet periods. Clients want to see "how is my case going", which software answers by reading court publications. The dominant small-firm tool, ADVBOX (R$ 220–1,800/month), sells on monitoring court notices through DJEN, the national electronic justice gazette, plus deadlines and tasks. It does not market a client portal ([ADVBOX, Aug 2026](https://advbox.com.br/blog/valor-da-advbox/)). EasyJur (about R$ 169/month per an aggregator) and Projuris (from about R$ 2,000/month) do market one ([EasyJur](https://easyjur.com/blog/?p=3093961); [B2B Stack](https://www.b2bstack.com.br/compare/easyjur-vs-projuris)). Astrea's prices could only be found on a third-party tracker, with no currency stated ([PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/aurum)). Brazil has **more than 1.3 million lawyers** ([Migalhas, Sep 2025](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/440932/sociedades-de-advogados-profissionalizacao-e-papel-da-oab)), but no national count of firms. The OAB advertising rule (Provimento 205/2021) allows discreet information channels and forbids active client-seeking ([Provimento text](https://www.conjur.com.br/dl/pr/provimento-2052021.pdf)). A private case page is not advertising. A public page promoting the firm could be read as advertising. The bigger issue is **professional secrecy**. One password per project, passed around a family or a company, is weaker than a login for each person, and notification e-mails must not reveal case details. That secrecy point is the researchers' own reading; no OAB opinion on client portals was found. Law is a later option for consulting and boutique firms (contracts, family, real estate), once Unio has per-person client logins.

## Professions that do not fit

### Accountants: a monthly checklist business already served by bundled portals

Accounting is a **recurring monthly loop**: the client sends statements, invoices and payroll changes, and the office returns tax-payment slips and reports. Chasing documents is the main pain point, and WhatsApp is the default channel ([Ledware, Aug 2026](https://www.ledware.com.br/2026/08/09/portal-do-cliente-contabil-documentos/)). Incumbents already sell exactly that: automatic recurring document requests with deadlines and status ([Acessórias Docs](https://acessorias.com/site/funcionalidade/acessorias-docs/)), a client portal plus app plus a shared WhatsApp inbox, with tracking of when the client downloaded a file ([Gestta](https://www.gestta.com.br/gestta/)), and a portal built into the Domínio tax system ([Onvio portal guide](https://suporte.dominioatendimento.com/ctsfiles/onvio_portal_cliente_escritorio.pdf?id=9420925&ds=centralSolucao)). Thomson Reuters owns Domínio, Onvio and Gestta ([Thomson Reuters, 2022](https://www.thomsonreuters.com.br/pt/sala-de-imprensa/thomson-reuters-anuncia-aquisicao-de-gestta.html)). A pricing guide for accountants' own clients says a document portal is usually included in the basic monthly package ([Contabilidade Zen](https://www.contabilidadezen.com.br/blog/quanto-custa-contador-precos-2026/)). The market is large: **528,627 accounting professionals** (Jan 2025) ([Jornal Contábil](https://jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/quantos-contadores-existem-no-brasil-em-2025/)) and about 82,000–100,000 firms, though both firm counts are estimates of uncertain date ([Roberto Dias Duarte](https://www.robertodiasduarte.com.br/relatorio-executivo-evolucao-das-organizacoes-contabeis-no-brasil-1995-2024/); [Contábeis](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/16964/contabilidade-no-brasil-possui-490-mil-profissionais/)). Unio would need monthly checklists, tax-payment slips with due dates and integration with the tax software, against well-funded tools offices already pay for. Accounting does teach one useful lesson: **upload links that need no account get more use than logins** ([Pasta Contábil](https://pastacontabil.com.br/blog/automacao-cobranca-documentos-clientes-contabilidade)).

### Photographers: the core job is a photo-selection gallery, sold almost free

A photographer's core client interaction is **selection**. The client marks yes, no or maybe on hundreds of images and comments on single files, downloads stay off until final delivery, and the chosen file names go back to Lightroom ([picdrop docs](https://www.picdrop.com/web/docs/ai/en/collaboration-mode/)). Brazil's local leader, Alboom Proof, has a permanent free plan and a paid entry at **R$ 29/month**, per a third-party comparison ([Memorize Clipe](https://memorizeclipe.com/blog/comparativo-plataformas-entrega-de-fotos-2026)). Users credit it with faster selections and more sales of extra photos ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/203005/Alboom-Proof/reviews/)). The count of "a little over 50 thousand" formal photographers comes from an undated page with no source ([Catraca Livre](https://catracalivre.com.br/criatividade/como-achar-um-fotografo/)). Building a gallery, and storing thousands of high-resolution images per job in Drive, would cost a lot for customers anchored at R$ 0–30.

### Nutritionists, personal trainers and psychologists: ongoing care, cheap specialised tools, sensitive data

All three are **ongoing relationships**, not projects. A nutritionist sees a patient every 15 days with returns within 30, keeps contact by WhatsApp and asks for a food diary ([Cronoshare](https://www.cronoshare.com.br/croner-2499997-ana-paula-m)), often selling packages ([geracontratos](https://geracontratos.com.br/modelo-contrato-nutricao)). Online trainers sell 1-, 3-, 6- or 12-month plans with check-ins and monthly progress photos ([MFIT](https://www.mfitpersonal.com.br/pages/assinaturas.html); [ST Coach Pro](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1666237428)). Psychologists bill per session. Their main record is confidential and stays with them, so almost nothing is "delivered" to the client ([CFP Res. 001/2009](https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=112145)).

Each profession has cheap tools with a patient or student app. Dietbox costs R$ 49.90/month for three months, then R$ 91.90, with Premium at R$ 133.90 ([Dietbox](https://beta.dietbox.me/)), and WebDiet is similar ([Flow Nutri, a competitor](https://flownutri.com/comparativos/dietbox-e-webdiet-para-estudante)). PsicoManager costs R$ 89–119/month with scheduling, billing, video sessions and a patient app ([PsicoManager](https://www.psicomanager.com.br/planos)). Trainer apps start free and run to about R$ 40/month: MFIT unlimited is R$ 39.90 ([MFIT](https://www.mfitpersonal.com.br/pages/assinaturas.html)). The Welltrainer comparison, written by a vendor that ranks itself first, lists at least eleven trainer apps ([Welltrainer](https://welltrainer.com.br/melhor-app-para-personal-trainer/)). Each tool contains a core engine Unio lacks: a food database and meal-plan calculator, an electronic record with video sessions, or an exercise library.

Regulation adds weight. LGPD treats health data as **sensitive personal data**. Processing needs specific consent or a listed exception ([Lei 13.709/2018](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm)), so Unio would become a processor of sensitive data, needing a data-processing agreement and stronger security. The nutrition council requires paper records to be kept at least 20 years after the last entry. Electronic records are kept permanently and may be deleted only 20 years after the last entry ([CFN Res. 594/2017](https://www.cfn.org.br/wp-content/uploads/resolucoes/resolucoes_old/Res_594_2017.htm)). The psychology council requires at least 5 years ([CFP Res. 001/2009](https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=112145)). Cloud storage is allowed but the professional remains responsible ([Confidata](https://confidata.com.br/blog/lgpd-saude-mental-psicologos-terapia-online), a secondary source). Keeping files in the office's own Drive fits "the professional holds custody", but share links for health files are a leak risk. The markets are large: 181,374 nutritionists in 2022 ([Câmara/CFN](https://www2.camara.leg.br/atividade-legislativa/comissoes/comissoes-permanentes/cdc/apresentacoes-em-eventos/apresentacoes-de-convidados-em-eventos-de-2022/solicitacao-de-exames-por-nutricionistas/apresentacao-cfn/view)), roughly 400k–550k psychologists in conflicting council figures ([CFP](https://site.cfp.org.br/o-exercicio-profissional-enquanto-trabalho/)), and 160k–500k physical-education professionals in conflicting sources ([ALPB](https://sapl.al.pb.leg.br/media/sapl/public/materialegislativa/2020/72591/72591_texto_integral.pdf); [Congresso em Foco](https://congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/11549/bolsonaro-sanciona-regulamentacao-da-educacao-fisica)). Most of them work alone, while Unio is built for an office with several staff. The only plausible niche is a multi-professional clinic or studio that wants a branded document page on top of its existing specialist tool, and that demand is unverified.

## What other companies' expansions teach

Software companies that moved into neighbouring professions mostly did it slowly or by buying a specialist. HoneyBook started with wedding vendors in 2013, widened to independent service professionals, and still "runs deepest in wedding photography" ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/honeybook/); [withorbital](https://withorbital.com/data/software/honeybook/)). Mindbody bought Booker (salons) for about US$ 150M rather than reworking its yoga-studio product ([Mindbody](https://www.mindbodyonline.com/company/press/mindbody-completes-acquisition-of-booker-software)). ServiceTitan bought landscaping and pest-control specialists because "the expertise is more specialized" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/06/30/servicetitan-acquires-aspire-to-move-into-landscaping-raises-200m-at-a-9-5b-valuation)). One operator warns that neighbouring markets "may be enticingly similar, but they are usually different markets" ([Practical Founders](https://practicalfounders.com/articles/multi-vertical-saas-companies-difficult-execute/)). Houzz Pro serves both designers and contractors, and rivals attack it as not "built solely for interior designers" ([DesignFiles](https://blog.designfiles.co/houzz-pro-alternatives/)).

On data structure, the pattern is consistent. Tools for professions with long client relationships put the **client as its own record with many projects**. Clio requires every legal matter to belong to a contact, with custom fields on both. Project templates fill in new projects, and template changes do not alter projects already created ([Clio help](https://help.clio.com/hc/articles/9285959663131)). Karbon generates repeating monthly work from templates, and users complain when template edits do not reach existing work ([Karbon community](https://community.karbonhq.com/ask-a-peer-6/feature-request-recurring-work-at-a-template-level-102?postid=256)). Unio's project types already copy into each project, so it should expect the same request to "update my existing projects".

## Checking the team's sketch against the evidence

The sketch in `docs/future-ideas.md` proposes a `vertical` on the tenant, one shared app with per-profession project types, stages and wording, a `Client` record with its own login, client uploads and measurements, deliverables published as versions, a `lifecycle` (staged or ongoing), tasks with someone responsible for engineers, LGPD care for health, and the order "Client plus vertical first, then one second profession with a real office". Most of it holds up. Some parts move.

**Confirmed: one app, shared core, the code keeps saying "project".** This is how HoneyBook handles industries, with templates and wording rather than separate products ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/honeybook/)). The professions worth pursuing all use the word "projeto" anyway. A `vertical` chosen at office creation is cheap, and its job is mostly to pick the starting project types, stage names and screen wording. For interiors and architect-led landscape, Unio's existing custom project types already cover most of it. The vertical makes setup quicker; it does not make anything newly possible.

**Confirmed with a refinement: the `Client` record.** A client as its own record, with many projects and optional custom fields, is the common pattern ([Clio](https://help.clio.com/hc/articles/9285959663131)). It is needed for repeat clients (developers, builders, architects hiring engineers), and for secrecy-sensitive professions. The refinement: **keep the no-login path.** Accounting vendors find that links needing no account get more use ([Pasta Contábil](https://pastacontabil.com.br/blog/automacao-cobranca-documentos-clientes-contabilidade)). Brazilian clients live in WhatsApp: 95% of companies use it per a 2023 survey ([Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2023/10/28/internet-e-redes-sociais/whatsapp-e-utilizado-por-95-das-empresas-brasileiras-mostra-pesquisa/)). Client login should therefore be an option per office or per project, next to today's password link, not a replacement.

**Moved up: client uploads, approval of individual files, outside collaborators, ART/RRT.** These serve the professions that fit. Engineers need files in from clients and partners. Interior and brand designers need approval of each file. Engineers need guest access for the architect-to-engineer channel. Engineers and architects both need technical-responsibility records.

**Moved down: `lifecycle` staged versus ongoing, and measurements.** Open-ended work mainly serves accountants, trainers, nutritionists and brand retainers. Charts of weight and body measurements serve only health and fitness. The evidence says not to pursue those markets now, so neither feature should come early.

**Challenged: tasks with someone responsible for engineers.** The engineering evidence points to **revision labels, approval status, ART tracking and a pending-issues list for coordination rounds**, not a general task system. Tasks with owners matter mainly on construction sites, which is the crowded category Unio should stay out of. A simple pending-issues list per project covers the design-office need.

**Confirmed: health and LGPD.** The concern is real, and the evidence goes further: health is not just risky but a poor fit. It should be a "no" for now, not a "later with care".

**Confirmed with a name: the second profession.** The sketch says "one second profession with a real office". The evidence names it: **interior designers**, then design-engineering offices.

## Product changes, in phases

Each phase lists what it changes and which professions it opens. Every new table belongs to one office, like the rest of Unio's data. It must follow the same per-office isolation rule, and its tests must prove that. Several of these changes alter settled decisions (client access, accounts for people outside the office), so under the project's rules the decision register (section 2 of `docs/multi-tenant-implementation-plan.md`) and `docs/decision-changelog.md` must be updated before coding each phase.

**Phase 1: profession on the office, and built-in project types per profession.** Add a `vertical` field to `Tenant`, chosen from a fixed list (architecture, interior design, landscape, engineering…) when the office is created. Each profession gets its own built-in project types and stage names: for interiors, briefing, preliminary study, detailed project, works follow-up; for engineering, the NBR 16636 stages plus ART issued, fire-brigade or utility approval and as-built. Screen wording changes where needed, while the code keeps saying "project". The example project shown to visitors should match the office's profession. Features switch on per office as decision A40 already allows. **Unlocks:** interior designers and architect-led landscape offices, fully. Engineering design offices and event planners can start with what exists.

**Phase 2: the client as a record, with optional login.** Replace `Project.client_name` with a `Client` record (name, contact details, optional custom fields) that can own many projects. Optionally allow linked people, such as a spouse or a company's partners. Client login is an option next to the per-project password, never a replacement. This is a significant change to who can sign in and must be agreed and recorded first. **Unlocks:** repeat clients for every profession, the per-person access that law would need, and the base for Phase 3.

**Phase 3: files flowing in, and approval of each file.** Add a client upload area or "document requests" per project, saved into the office's Drive the same way Unio already organises files. The upload should work through a link with no account. Each file version gets a status (sent, approved, changes requested), client comments, an optional revision label (R0, R1…) and an "issued for approval / issued for construction" status. **Unlocks:** engineers (base drawings, soil tests, approvals), interior designers (approving layout and 3D), brand designers (feedback rounds), and architects themselves.

**Phase 4: the engineering pack and outside collaborators.** Add a technical-responsibility record per project (ART or RRT: number, type, discipline, responsible professional, status, PDF), shown on the client page. Add a simple pending-issues list per project for coordination rounds. Add **outside collaborators**: a guest login for another office's professional inside one project, limited to their discipline's folder. Guests should stay inside the architect's office at first; true sharing of a project between two offices is a larger architectural change, which can come later if guests prove the channel. **Unlocks:** design-engineering offices as customers, and the architect-to-engineer acquisition channel.

**Phase 5, only if Phase 1–4 customers ask: item lists and ongoing work.** A selection list per project, with items, supplier, price and per-item client approval, serves interior shopping lists, landscape plant lists and event supplier lists in one feature. An event budget (planned, contracted, paid) and guest list would sit on top for planners. A project `lifecycle` of "ongoing", with recurring deliveries, serves brand retainers. **Unlocks:** deeper interiors, event planners, brand retainers.

**Not planned:** body measurements and charts, health records, scheduling with video sessions, monthly accounting checklists, court-progress feeds, photo-selection galleries and construction-site diaries. Each one is the core product of a cheap, entrenched competitor, and none serves the professions that fit.

## Risks

The first risk is **dilution**. Products that serve two audiences get attacked by "built only for X" competitors, as Houzz Pro is ([DesignFiles](https://blog.designfiles.co/houzz-pro-alternatives/)). Unio's strength with architects comes from architecture-specific detail. Every profession added means more templates, wording and support questions; no data on support cost per added profession was found. The second risk is **false similarity**. Neighbouring professions often turn out to be different markets ([Practical Founders](https://practicalfounders.com/articles/multi-vertical-saas-companies-difficult-execute/)), and no source here proves that Brazilian interior designers or engineers want a client portal enough to pay for it. The third is **storage**: large BIM models, and offices that keep files outside Google Drive, could clash with Unio's Drive-only design. The fourth is **access and privacy**. Client logins and guest collaborators widen who can see an office's data. Any move toward law or health raises the bar to per-person access and secrecy-grade notifications, and in health to sensitive-data processing under LGPD. The fifth is **price**. Several professions anchor their software spend at R$ 0–140 a month, so a price that suits architecture offices would look expensive to solo practitioners.

## Open questions to validate with real offices

Talk to three to five interior design offices, including some run by non-architects. Do they pay for any management or client tool today? Do their clients expect a page, or is WhatsApp plus a Drive folder enough? How do they handle product selections and the "reserva técnica" commission? What would they pay compared with architects? Talk to two or three structural or MEP offices that work with Unio's architect customers. Who pays them, the owner or the architect? Where do their files live, Drive, a server or Autodesk? Would they accept a guest login inside the architect's project? Would they later pay for their own office? Ask current architect customers how many engineering offices they work with in a year, which sizes the channel. Check whether Unio's Drive organisation copes with the file sizes engineers send. For event planners, a single conversation would show whether the supplier list and budget matter enough, and whether anyone would pay above the R$ 99 Notion template. Before any step toward law or health, a lawyer should confirm what LGPD and professional-secrecy rules would require of Unio. The research did not read the full texts of the newer health-council rules or find any OAB opinion on client portals.

## Conclusion

The question was "could Unio serve other professions". The more useful question is **which professions already think in projects**. Interior designers, architect-led landscape offices and design-engineering offices do, and Unio can serve them with configuration plus three features that also improve the product for architects: files coming in, approval of each file, and outside collaborators. The professions that think in sessions, months or galleries would need Unio to become a different product, in markets where the specialist tools cost about as much as a lunch. Engineers are the most strategic addition. They fit, they have no client-facing tool, and Unio's architect customers already work with them, so the best way to reach them may run through the architects Unio already serves.
